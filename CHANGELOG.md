## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-26

### Added
- `agentforge doctor [--json] [--strict]`: audits `.agentforge.yaml` validity, detects missing or drifted target rule files, and summarizes rule health score with actionable fixes.
- `codex` (`AGENTS.md`) and `gemini` (`GEMINI.md`) sync targets.
